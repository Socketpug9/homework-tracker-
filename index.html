    home reminders / shortcuts

    Remind me when I get home.

    One automation in the Shortcuts app (already on your iPhone). It runs by itself every time you arrive home.

    1. Open Shortcuts → tap Automation at the bottom → + (or New Automation).
    2. Choose Arrive. Set Location to Home (your address from your contact card). Pick Run Immediately and turn Notify When Run off. Tap Next.
    3. Tap New Blank Automation, then add these 3 actions in order:
      1 / show notification
      2 / wait1800 seconds (30 min)
      3 / show notification
    4. Tap Done. That's it. Tapping one of these notifications opens Shortcuts, so open Homework from your Home Screen.
    Notification texttap to copy

    Tip: if “Home” isn't offered in step 2, open the Contacts app → your own card → add your home address.
